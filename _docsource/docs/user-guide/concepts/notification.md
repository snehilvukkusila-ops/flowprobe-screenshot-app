## What it is

The **Notifications** screen shows the list of notifications, each with its Read or Unread status.

![The Notifications screen](/screenshots/flowprobe-screenshot-app-notifications-1/0.png)

## Where to find it

Open `/flowprobe-screenshot-app/notifications`.

## Statuses you may see

- **Unread**
- **Read**

These are the values seen on this screen; others may exist.

## Actions on this screen

- **Mark all read**
