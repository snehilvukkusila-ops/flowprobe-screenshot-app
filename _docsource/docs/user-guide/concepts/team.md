## What it is

The **Team** screen shows a table of your team members with `Name`, `Role` and `Status` columns.

![The Team screen](/screenshots/flowprobe-screenshot-app-team-1/0.png)

## Where to find it

Open `/flowprobe-screenshot-app/team`.

## On this screen

- **Name**
- **Role**
- **Status**

## Statuses you may see

- **Active**
- **Away**
- **Offline**

These are the values seen on this screen; others may exist.

## Actions on this screen

- **+ Invite**
- **View activity**
