## What it is

Manage account preferences covers the **Team dashboard** and **Settings** screens.

The **Team dashboard** screen shows a **Refresh** control that runs a Refresh action.

:::note
This statement could not be confirmed against the project's own sources. Treat it as unverified.
:::

The **Settings** screen shows a **Save** control that runs a Save action; **Save** was disabled when the screen was captured, before its inputs were filled.

:::note
This statement could not be confirmed against the project's own sources. Treat it as unverified.
:::

## Where to find it

Open `/flowprobe-screenshot-app/dashboard`.

## What you can do

- [Team dashboard](../concepts/dashboard.md)
- [Settings](../concepts/setting.md)
