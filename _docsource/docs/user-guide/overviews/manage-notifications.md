## What it is

Manage Notifications shows the status badges and actions for your notifications.

The **Notifications** screen shows `Unread` and `Read` status badges and a **Mark all read** control.

## Where to find it

Open `/flowprobe-screenshot-app/notifications`.

## What you can do

- [Notifications](../concepts/notification.md)
- [Mark all read](../tasks/mark-all-read.md)
