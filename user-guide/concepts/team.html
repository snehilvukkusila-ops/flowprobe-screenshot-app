Team

Browse the team page to view each member's name, presence status (Active, Away, Offline), role, and the projects they own, with a + Invite action to add new teammates.

What it is

The Team screen shows a table of your team members with Name, Role and Status columns.

The Team screen

Where to find it

Open /flowprobe-screenshot-app/team.

On this screen

  • Name
  • Role
  • Status

Statuses you may see

  • Active
  • Away
  • Offline

These are the values seen on this screen; others may exist.

Actions on this screen

  • + Invite
  • View activity
