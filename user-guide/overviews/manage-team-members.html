Manage team members overview

Where to find Manage team members and what you can do with it.

What it is

Manage team members is the feature where you see the people in your team and invite new ones.

The Team screen shows the Name, Role, and Status columns and the + Invite control.

The Team member screen shows the View activity control and Active and done badges.

Where to find it

Open /flowprobe-screenshot-app/team.

What you can do

  • Team
  • Invite team
